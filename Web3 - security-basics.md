# Web3 Security Basics

Security is one of the most important parts of Web3.

## Basic Tips

- Never share your seed phrase.
- Always verify wallet connection requests.
- Double-check URLs before signing transactions.
- Enable two-factor authentication where available.
- Keep your wallet and browser updated.

## Why It Matters

Good security habits help protect your digital assets and build confidence while exploring the Web3 ecosystem.

Keep learning. Stay secure.
