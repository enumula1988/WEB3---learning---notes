# Seed Phrase Explained

A seed phrase is a sequence of words used to recover a crypto wallet.

## Why It Matters

The seed phrase gives access to the wallet and its assets. Anyone who gets the seed phrase may be able to control the wallet.

## Basic Safety Rules

- Never share your seed phrase with anyone.
- Never store it in screenshots, chats, or public cloud storage.
- Write it down and keep it in a secure offline location.
- Be careful with websites or people asking you to enter your seed phrase.
- A legitimate support team should never ask for your seed phrase.

## Key Takeaway

Your wallet address can be shared to receive assets, but your seed phrase must remain private.
