\# Gas Fees Explained



Gas fees are the network fees paid to process transactions on a blockchain.



\## Why do we pay gas?



Blockchains use network resources to process and verify transactions. Gas fees help compensate the network for this work.



\## What affects gas fees?



Gas costs can change depending on:



\- Network activity

\- Transaction complexity

\- Blockchain design



\## Simple example



Sending a token may require a network fee. Interacting with a smart contract can require more computation and therefore may use more gas.



\## Key takeaway



Gas is essentially the cost of using blockchain network resources.



Before making a transaction, always check the network and the expected fee.
