# Gas Fees Explained

Gas fees are the network fees paid to process transactions on a blockchain.

## Why Do We Pay Gas?

Blockchains use network resources to process and verify transactions. Gas fees help compensate the network for this work.

## What Affects Gas Fees?

Gas costs can change depending on:

- Network activity
- Transaction complexity
- Blockchain design

## Simple Example

Sending a token may require a network fee. When the network is busy, the fee can increase because more users are competing for limited block space.

## Key Takeaway

Gas fees are an important part of how blockchain networks process transactions and maintain security.
