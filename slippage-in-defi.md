\# What Is Slippage in DeFi?



Slippage is the difference between the price a user expects to receive for a trade and the actual price received when the transaction is executed.



\## Why Does Slippage Happen?



Slippage can happen because of:



\- Price changes while a transaction is being processed

\- Low liquidity in a trading pool

\- Large trades that move the market price

\- High market volatility



\## Example



Suppose you expect to receive 100 USDC for your tokens.



If the trade executes and you receive only 98 USDC, the difference is caused by slippage.



\## Slippage Tolerance



Slippage tolerance is the maximum price difference a user is willing to accept for a swap.



For example, a 1% slippage tolerance means the user accepts up to a 1% difference from the expected trade price.



\## Why Is Slippage Important?



Understanding slippage helps DeFi users make safer swaps and avoid unexpected losses, especially when trading large amounts or using low-liquidity pools.



\## Conclusion



Slippage is a normal part of DeFi trading, but users should always check liquidity, market conditions, and slippage tolerance before confirming a swap.
