# Reverse Auction Basics

A reverse auction is a mechanism where sellers compete by offering more competitive prices to a buyer.

In a token buyback, participants can submit the amount of tokens they want to sell along with their asking price.

After the submission period ends, valid offers can be reviewed and the most competitive offers can be prioritized based on the available budget.

## Key Points

- Sellers submit the amount they want to sell.
- Sellers specify their asking price.
- Offers are reviewed after the submission period.
- Competitive offers can be prioritized.
- Selected sellers receive payment according to the buyback rules.

## Why It Matters

A reverse auction can provide a structured way to allocate a limited buyback budget while allowing participants to compete through their offered prices.

This concept is useful for understanding how some blockchain projects can organize token buyback programs in a transparent and systematic way.
