# Stablecoins Explained

Stablecoins are crypto assets designed to maintain a relatively stable value.

## Common Types

- Fiat-backed stablecoins are backed by reserves such as USD.
- Crypto-backed stablecoins use other crypto assets as collateral.
- Algorithmic stablecoins use rules or mechanisms to target price stability.

## Why Stablecoins Matter

Stablecoins are commonly used for trading, payments, liquidity, and moving value across blockchain networks.

## Key Risk

Stablecoins can face risks such as depegging, reserve issues, liquidity problems, or smart contract failures.

## Key Takeaway

Stablecoins provide a stable-value option within the highly volatile crypto ecosystem.
